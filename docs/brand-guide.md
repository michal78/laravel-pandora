# Laravel Pandora visual identity

## Brand idea

Pandora is represented by an open dimensional box. The box communicates a framework that contains tools, agents, memory, providers, and workflows. The light emerging from its center represents controlled capability being released into a Laravel application.

The identity stays close to the existing Claude-generated interface: restrained violet accents, spacious white surfaces in light mode, near-black surfaces in dark mode, soft lavender selection states, and rounded components.

## Primary marks

- `logos/laravel-pandora-light.svg`: full lockup for light backgrounds.
- `logos/laravel-pandora-dark.svg`: full lockup for dark backgrounds.
- `logos/laravel-pandora-compact-*.svg`: no tagline.
- `logos/sidebar-lockup.svg`: compact navigation branding.
- `icons/svg/pandora-icon.svg`: primary standalone icon.
- `icons/svg/pandora-icon-mono.svg`: monochrome/currentColor icon.

## Typography

Use Inter as the preferred interface and brand typeface. Fall back to the native system sans-serif stack.

Recommended weights:

- 400: body copy
- 500: navigation and labels
- 600: headings
- 700: brand name and important actions

## Shape language

- Default radius: 14px
- Large cards: 20px
- Buttons: 12 to 14px
- Avoid excessive pill shapes except segmented controls and compact status labels.
- Use thin neutral borders. Do not use strong drop shadows in dark mode.

## Logo rules

- Preserve clear space equal to one quarter of the icon width.
- Do not recolor individual cube faces outside the supplied palette.
- Do not add the Laravel logo inside the Pandora icon.
- Do not use the icon smaller than 20px in UI.
- At 16px, use the simplified favicon.
- Use the full wordmark above 260px available width.
- Use the sidebar lockup or standalone icon in constrained navigation.

## Voice

Technical, calm, capable, transparent, and security-aware. Avoid mystical fantasy language in product UI. “Pandora” is the brand, while the product language should describe concrete agents, runs, tools, approvals, memory, and automations.
